Bruker spectrometers use [SpecMan4EPR](https://specman4epr.com/index.html) for data acquisition and spectrometer control. The software is developed by [Prof. Boris Epel](https://www.nist.gov/people/boris-epel) and used by many EPR labs [around the world](https://specman4epr.com/application.html).
