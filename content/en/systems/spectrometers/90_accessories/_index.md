This section covers optional accessories for the EPR spectrometer systems.

- **ENDOR Extension** — Electron-Nuclear Double Resonance (ENDOR) adds a radiofrequency (RF) channel to the EPR spectrometer, allowing simultaneous excitation of electron and nuclear spin transitions. ENDOR spectroscopy provides hyperfine coupling information with high resolution and is used to study the local nuclear environment of paramagnetic centers.
