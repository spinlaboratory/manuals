High-power microwave amplifiers are required for pulsed EPR spectroscopy to generate the short, intense microwave pulses needed for spin inversion and echo formation. Modern solid-state amplifiers have largely replaced traveling-wave tube (TWT) amplifiers due to their compact size, reliability, and ease of use.

The following amplifier is documented in this section:

- **40 W Q-Band Amplifier (AMP-Q40)** — A 40 W solid-state microwave amplifier operating at Q-Band (~35 GHz). Designed as a direct TWT replacement for Q-Band pulsed EPR spectrometers. Compatible with the X-Band IF system and the QLP probe head.

For questions about microwave amplifiers for your system contact epr-applications@bruker.com.
