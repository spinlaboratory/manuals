Magnetic field generation is a central component of any EPR spectrometer. This section covers the superconducting magnet systems used in Bruker EPR spectrometers.

**Superconducting magnets** are used in Q-Band and high-field EPR spectrometers. They provide highly stable, high-field operation with very low drift rates, particularly when operated in persistent mode. The superconducting magnets used in Bruker instruments are manufactured by [CRYOGENIC Ltd.](http://www.cryogenic.co.uk/) and are cooled by a closed-cycle helium refrigerator (cryocooler), requiring no liquid cryogens.

This section covers:
- **Superconducting Magnets** — Installation, cooldown, warmup, and probe installation procedures for the cryogen-free superconducting magnet system.
