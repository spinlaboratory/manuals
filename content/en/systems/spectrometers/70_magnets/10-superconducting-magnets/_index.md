Many Bruker (high-field) EPR spectrometers use superconducting magnets manufactured by [CRYOGENIC LTD](http://www.cryogenic.co.uk/). The magnet system comes with extensive documentation.

All information provided here is specific to superconducting magnets specifically manufactured to be used in Bruker instruments. In addition, most instructions here describe procedures for day-to-day operations.
