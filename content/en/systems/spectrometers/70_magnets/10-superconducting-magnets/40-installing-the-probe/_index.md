This section describes how to install the EPR probe into the superconducting magnet of the Compact-Q spectrometer. The procedure assumes the magnet is at its base temperature and the iVTI is running.

{{% pageinfo color="warning" %}}
### Warning
Never insert the probe into the cryostat without a flow of helium purge gas running. Air entering the sample space will freeze and block the cryostat, requiring a full warmup to clear.
{{% /pageinfo %}}

{{% pageinfo color="primary" %}}
### Important
Before inserting the probe, make sure the probe is at room temperature or has been precooled carefully. Inserting a warm probe too quickly into the cold cryostat may cause thermal stress or a sudden pressure rise in the cryostat.
{{% /pageinfo %}}

## Installing the Probe

1. Ensure the iVTI is running and the cryostat has reached its operating temperature.
2. Open the helium purge gas valve and charge the static column (the sample space) to slightly above atmospheric pressure with clean helium gas (grade 5). Maintain a gentle flow of helium during probe insertion to prevent air from entering.
3. Remove the blind plug (if installed) from the top of the cryostat by loosening the securing nut and pulling it out.
4. Carefully lower the probe into the cryostat. Guide the WR-28 waveguide connection at the bottom of the probe into the waveguide opening at the base of the static column. Do not force the probe — if you feel resistance, withdraw the probe, check the alignment, and try again.
5. Once the probe is fully seated, connect the WR-28 waveguide flange at the bottom of the probe to the spectrometer waveguide. Tighten the flange screws finger-tight first, then tighten evenly with a wrench.
6. Connect the temperature sensor cable from the probe to the temperature controller.
7. Tighten the probe securing nut at the top of the cryostat. This nut is hand-tight only — do not use tools.
8. Close the helium purge gas valve.
9. If the probe mounting bracket is used, install it now following the instructions in the [Probe Mounting Bracket](/accessories/mounting_bracket/) section.
10. Start the diaphragm pump and open the valve to evacuate the static column. Lower the pressure to approximately 200–500 mbar.
11. Close the valve and turn off the pump.

The probe is now installed. Proceed to insert a sample following the instructions in the [iVTI Operation — Changing the Sample](/systems/spectrometers/80-integrated-vti/30-vti-operation/#changing-the-sample) section.

## Removing the Probe

To remove the probe from the cryostat:

1. Remove the sample from the probe.
2. Insert a blind plug into the sample opening at the top of the probe.
3. Open the static column to atmospheric pressure by slowly opening the valve to the helium purge gas supply.
4. Disconnect the waveguide flange at the bottom of the probe.
5. Disconnect the temperature sensor cable.
6. Loosen the probe securing nut and slowly pull the probe out of the cryostat. Maintain helium purge gas flow during removal.
7. Once the probe is removed, insert the blind plug into the cryostat opening to prevent air ingress.
8. Close the purge gas valve.
