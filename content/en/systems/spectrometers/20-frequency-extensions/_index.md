The Bridge12 X-Band IF system is a modular platform that can be extended to operate at frequencies other than X-Band (8–12 GHz). Frequency extensions convert the microwave signals generated by the X-Band IF system to higher or lower frequency bands. Each extension includes the necessary waveguide components, circulators, low-noise amplifiers, and active multiplier or mixer chains required for EPR spectroscopy at that frequency.

The following frequency extensions are available:

- **S-Band Extension** — Extends operation to S-Band (~2.7–3.9 GHz, WR-284 waveguide).
- **Q-Band Extension** — Extends operation to Q-Band (~34 GHz, WR-28 waveguide). Required for use with Q-Band EPR probes such as the QLP probe head.
- **W-Band Extension** — Extends operation to W-Band (~94 GHz, WR-10 waveguide).
- **High-Field EPR** — Operation at very high magnetic fields and correspondingly high microwave frequencies (> 100 GHz).

For questions about frequency extensions for your specific system, contact epr-applications@bruker.com.
