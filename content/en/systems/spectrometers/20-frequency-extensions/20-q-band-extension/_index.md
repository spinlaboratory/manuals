The Q-Band frequency extension converts the X-Band IF system to operate at Q-Band microwave frequencies (~34–36 GHz). Q-Band EPR spectroscopy offers higher sensitivity compared to X-Band due to the larger Boltzmann population difference at higher frequencies and improved spectral resolution for samples with g-anisotropy or hyperfine structure.

The Q-Band extension includes an active multiplier chain, a circulator, a low-noise preamplifier, and WR-28 waveguide components. It connects to the PFU TX and SIG ports of the X-Band IF system. In combination with the [QLP EPR probe head](/probes/qlp-probes/) and the [40 W Q-Band amplifier](/systems/spectrometers/30-amplifiers/20-q-band-amplifier/), the Q-Band extension forms the microwave backbone of the Compact-Q spectrometer platform.

Key specifications:
- Operating frequency: 33–36 GHz
- Waveguide standard: WR-28
- Compatible probes: QLP-1.6, QLP-2.5
