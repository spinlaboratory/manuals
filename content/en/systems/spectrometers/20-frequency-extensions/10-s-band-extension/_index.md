The S-Band frequency extension converts the X-Band IF system to operate at S-Band microwave frequencies (~2.7–3.95 GHz). S-Band EPR spectroscopy is particularly useful for samples with broad EPR linewidths, aqueous biological samples, or in-vivo applications where the reduced microwave absorption of water at lower frequencies is advantageous.

The S-Band extension connects to the X-Band IF system via the back panel and provides the full down-conversion and up-conversion chain required to generate and detect microwave pulses at S-Band frequencies. The waveguide standard for S-Band is WR-284.

For further information contact epr-applications@bruker.com.
