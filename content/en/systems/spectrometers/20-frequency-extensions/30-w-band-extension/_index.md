The W-Band frequency extension converts the X-Band IF system to operate at W-Band microwave frequencies (~75–110 GHz, center frequency ~94 GHz). W-Band EPR spectroscopy provides significantly improved g-factor resolution compared to lower-frequency bands, making it well suited for resolving overlapping radical species and for orientation-selective DEER experiments.

The W-Band extension uses an active multiplier chain to generate microwave signals in the W-Band frequency range from the X-Band IF local oscillator. The waveguide standard for W-Band is WR-10. A dedicated circulator and low-noise amplifier are included in the extension module.

W-Band operation typically requires a high-field superconducting magnet capable of generating fields of approximately 3.3 T at 94 GHz.
