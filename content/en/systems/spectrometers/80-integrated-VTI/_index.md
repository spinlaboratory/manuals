The integrated Variable Temperature Insert (iVTI) is a liquid cryogen-free cryostat built into the superconducting magnet of the Compact-Q EPR spectrometer. It enables continuous low-temperature EPR measurements down to approximately 1.8 K without the need for liquid helium.

The iVTI operates by circulating high-purity helium gas through a closed-loop circuit cooled by the same cryocooler that maintains the superconducting magnet. Temperature regulation is achieved via a feedback-controlled heater and is monitored using the calibrated temperature sensor integrated into the EPR probe head.
