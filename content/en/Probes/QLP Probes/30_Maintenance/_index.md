In general, the QLP EPR probe is completely maintenance free under normal operating conditions. No lubrication, recalibration, or consumable replacement is required during routine use.

## Cleaning

The exterior of the probe can be cleaned with a dry or slightly damp lint-free cloth. Do not use solvents or cleaning agents that may damage the probe body or the waveguide connections.

## Sample Holder and Sample Stick

The sample holder and sample stick may require periodic cleaning if sample material has been spilled inside the sample tube. To clean the sample holder:

1. Remove the sample stick from the probe.
2. Wipe the inside of the sample holder with a lint-free swab or tissue.
3. Make sure no sample material is left inside the resonator before reinserting the sample stick.

See the [Sample Holder](/probes/sample-holder/) section for information on the sample stick assembly and consumables.

## WR-28 Waveguide Connection

The WR-28 waveguide connection at the bottom of the probe should be kept free of dust and debris. If the waveguide flange becomes contaminated, clean it carefully with a lint-free cloth. Do not touch the interior of the waveguide with metal tools.

## Iris Mechanism

The iris adjustment mechanism (micrometer screw at the top of the probe) does not require lubrication. Do not force the iris mechanism, especially at cryogenic temperatures, as this can cause permanent damage to the iris drive.

## Contacting Support

If you suspect a problem with the probe, contact epr-applications@bruker.com and provide the probe serial number and a description of the issue.
