The Sample Stick described in this section is used for most Bruker EPR probes (and some DNP probes).

{{% pageinfo color="primary" %}}
### Important

Please, first make yourself familiar with the documentation of the Sample Stick before attempting to load a sample into the probe. All Bruker probes come with a standard sample (typically BDPA in polystyrene) to test the probe at room temperature. This is also a great sample to get familiar with the Sample Stick.
{{% /pageinfo %}}

{{% pageinfo color="primary" %}}
### New Sample Holder (Spring 2023)

Starting 2023, all Bruker EPR probes are shipped with an updated version of the sample holder. If the description provided here does not exactly match the sample holder you have and you have additional questions, feel free to reach out to Bruker at epr-applications@bruker.com and inquire about specific instructions for your sample holder.
{{% /pageinfo %}}
