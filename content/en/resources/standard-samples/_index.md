Standard samples are used in EPR spectroscopy to calibrate and test spectrometers, optimize pulse parameters, and verify spectrometer performance. A good EPR standard sample should be chemically stable, have a well-characterized EPR spectrum, and provide a sufficiently strong signal at the frequencies and temperatures of interest.

This section provides preparation instructions for the following standard samples:

- **BDPA in Polystyrene (BDPA/PS)** — A stable organic radical co-dissolved in polystyrene. BDPA/PS is one of the most widely used EPR standards across all frequency bands. It exhibits a narrow, nearly isotropic EPR line near g = 2.003 and is suitable for calibrating pulse parameters, measuring B₁ field homogeneity, and testing spectrometer performance at temperatures from room temperature down to liquid helium temperatures.
